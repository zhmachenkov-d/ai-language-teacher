# Capture — lesson screen

## Composition
- Center: dialogue / voice (teacher ↔ learner)
- Peripheral: lesson plan (side or top)

## Mic
- Always on screen
- Default: push-to-talk / hold
- Settings: optional auto-listen after teacher turn

## Plan placement
- Left sidebar: stage/step list

## Dialogue form
- Chat transcript with teacher / learner bubbles

## Pronunciation feedback
- Inline under learner bubble

## TTS
- Play on teacher bubble and on material cards

## Exit / pause
- Header: «Завершить урок» / «Пауза»
- Window close mid-lesson: save history, resumable
- Status: «В процессе» / «Не завершен» (confirm preferred label)

## Incomplete status label
- «Не завершен»

## Listening-check
- Modalities: choice in chat AND voice answer/retell; template/teacher picks per step
- Player: play on card/bubble + progress/scrubber + seek/replay + pause

## Listening choice + transcript
- Choice: separate A/B/C card
- Audio text: hidden until after answer
